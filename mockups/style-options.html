Ten directions for the site. Every mockup has the same pieces: the game wall with each pick's case, a hand-drawn card for who recommended it, the 🥵 meter that intensifies with votes, the shame pile, and the buddy-list messenger on the right. Covers and games here are made up; the real site pulls cover art from the game database. On a phone the messenger moves to its own tab. Pick a number, or mix pieces from a few.
